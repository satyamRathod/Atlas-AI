# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## 1. At-Will Employment & Classification
All employment at Acme Corp is at-will. This means either the company or the employee can terminate the relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. Employees are classified as either Exempt or Non-Exempt under the FLSA. Non-exempt employees must log all hours in TimeClockX daily and are eligible for overtime (1.5x base pay) for hours worked over 40 in a single workweek.

## 2. Core Working Hours & Remote Work
Our core collaborative hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST. All employees, including fully remote staff, must be reachable via Slack during these hours. 
*   **Hybrid Policy:** Employees attached to a physical hub must work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
*   **Stipend:** Remote employees receive a monthly $50 USD stipend for internet expenses, processed on the final pay cycle of each month.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave
Acme Corp offers 20 days of Accrued PTO annually, calculated at a rate of 1.66 days per month. 
*   **Rollover:** A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can roll over into the next calendar year. Any remaining unused balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st.
*   **Bereavement Leave:** Employees are eligible for up to 3 consecutive days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.

## 4. Hardware and Cybersecurity
The company provides a standard hardware package (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad). 
*   **VPN:** Employees must connect to the GlobalShield VPN when working on public Wi-Fi.
*   **Passwords:** Passwords must be updated every 90 days via 1Password and contain at least 14 characters, including one special symbol.
