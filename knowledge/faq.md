# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - CloudSync Platform

### Q1: How do I reset my CloudSync account password?
Navigate to the login screen and click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email address. You will receive a secure token link valid for 15 minutes. If the link expires, you must request a new one.

### Q2: What are the limits on the Free Tier plan?
The Free Tier includes:
*   Up to 5 GB of total cloud storage.
*   A maximum file upload size of 250 MB per file.
*   An API limit of 100 requests per hour.

### Q3: Why am I seeing a "Sync Error 403" message?
Error 403 indicates an authentication failure. This usually happens if your system clock is out of sync with the CloudSync server by more than 5 minutes, or if your session token has expired. Log out, update your device's time settings to "Set Automatically," and log back in.

### Q4: Can I share my subscription with team members?
Yes, but only on the Pro or Enterprise plans. The Individual plan is limited to one concurrent login session. The Pro plan allows up to 5 team members, while the Enterprise plan supports unlimited users with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
