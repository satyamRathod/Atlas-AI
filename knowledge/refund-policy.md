# Apex Retail Refund and Return Policy

## 1. Standard Return Window
Customers may return items within 30 days of the original purchase date for a full refund to the original payment method, subject to the conditions below.

## 2. Condition Categories
To qualify for a full refund, items must be in their original state:
*   **Category A (Apparel):** Unworn, unwashed, and with all original tags attached.
*   **Category B (Electronics):** Unopened, factory seal intact. Opened electronics incur a 15% restocking fee.
*   **Category C (Final Sale):** Clearance items, software downloads, and gift cards are strictly non-refundable.

## 3. Processing Times and Exceptions
*   **Credit Cards:** Refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear on the statement once approved.
*   **Cash Purchases:** Returns exceeding $200 USD originally paid in cash will be refunded via a corporate check mailed within 14 business days.
*   **Shipping Costs:** Original shipping and handling fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to an Apex Retail error (e.g., shipping the incorrect item).
